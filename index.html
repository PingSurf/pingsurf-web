Zero-Effort Google Visibility

Most businesses don't even know this exists - it's costing them customers

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is the free listing that shows up whenever someone searches your business name, or searches nearby like "biryani near me" or "clinic near me." It's what people see on Google Search and Google Maps before they ever visit your website. An empty or inactive one quietly sends customers to your competitors instead.

A real example

Say two restaurants sit on the same street. Someone nearby searches "biryani near me" on their phone.

Restaurant A has a bare listing - no recent activity, no ordering option visible. It shows up, but the customer has to search further, call, or simply pick someone else.

Restaurant B runs PingSurf. Its listing shows an active "Order Food" button right on the Google result, synced automatically. The customer taps once and orders - no website, no phone call, no waiting.

Same street. Same search. One of them just made the sale before the customer even left Google.

How PingSurf handles this for you

Instant sync to Search and Maps

Every update you make in PingSurf reflects on your Google listing automatically - no separate GBP dashboard to fight with.

Buttons enabled automatically

PingSurf auto-activates the right GMB service options - like food ordering or appointment booking - so the buttons render instantly on Google, with nothing for you to configure manually.
