Our Mission

Empowering small to medium-sized businesses with intelligent automation.

PingSurf was built to solve a simple yet frustrating problem: small to medium-sized business owners spend too much time fighting clunky software and too little time growing their business.

Why We Built PingSurf

Whether you run a bustling local restaurant, a neighborhood medical clinic, or a retail shop, your customers expect instant replies, seamless booking, and accurate information online 24/7.

Traditional marketing tools are built for massive corporations with dedicated IT teams. PingSurf changes that by turning simple WhatsApp chats into powerful publishing workflows, pairing them with AI RAG vector search, and directly connecting them to Google Business Profile and local booking hubs.

Built on Three Pillars

  • 01.
    Simplicity: If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can manage your entire digital presence. No complex dashboards required.
  • 02.
    Local Visibility: Automatically syncing with Google Maps and Search so nearby customers find you instantly.
  • 03.
    Absolute Trust: Bank-grade Fernet encryption protecting all customer data, orders, and appointment records at rest.
